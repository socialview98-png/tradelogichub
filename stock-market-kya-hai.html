Introduction

Agar aap Share Market ke baare me seekhna shuru kar rahe hain, to sabse pehla sawal hota hai ki Stock Market kya hota hai aur ye kaise kaam karta hai.

Stock Market ek aisa platform hai jahan companies ke shares ko buy aur sell kiya jata hai. Yahan investors companies me paisa invest karke unke growth ka hissa ban sakte hain.

Is article me hum beginner-friendly language me samjhenge ki Stock Market kaise kaam karta hai, shares kya hote hain aur investors market se kaise jude hote hain.

Stock Market Kya Hota Hai?

Stock Market ek financial marketplace hai jahan listed companies ke shares trade hote hain. Jab kisi company ko business grow karne ke liye paisa chahiye hota hai, to wo apni ownership ke kuch parts shares ke form me investors ko offer karti hai.

Jo log in shares ko kharidte hain wo company ke chhote hissedar ban jate hain.

Shares Kya Hote Hain?

Share ka matlab hota hai company ki ownership ka ek chhota hissa.

Example ke liye agar ABCD Company ne apni ownership ko chhote-chhote parts me divide kiya hai, to har part ko share kaha ja sakta hai.

Jab investor share kharidta hai to wo company ki growth me indirectly participate karta hai.

Stock Exchange Ka Role

Stock Exchange ek organised marketplace hota hai jahan buyers aur sellers shares ko buy aur sell karte hain.

Stock Exchange ensure karta hai ki trading ek proper system ke through, rules aur regulations ke according ho.

NSE (National Stock Exchange)

NSE India ka ek bada stock exchange hai jahan bahut saari listed companies ke shares trade hote hain.

BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange)

BSE duniya ke sabse purane stock exchanges me se ek hai. Yahan bhi companies ke shares ki buying aur selling hoti hai.

Stock Market Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

Stock Market me companies, investors aur brokers milkar kaam karte hain.

  • Company apne shares investors ke liye available karti hai.
  • Investor broker ke platform ke through shares buy ya sell karta hai.
  • Broker investor ke order ko Stock Exchange tak pahunchata hai.
  • Stock Exchange transaction ko complete karta hai.

Primary Market Aur Secondary Market

Primary Market

Jab koi company pehli baar apne shares public ko offer karti hai, to use Primary Market kaha jata hai.

Is process ko IPO (Initial Public Offering) kaha jata hai.

Secondary Market

Jab investors already listed shares ko ek dusre se buy aur sell karte hain, to use Secondary Market kaha jata hai.

Bull Market Aur Bear Market

Bull Market

Jab market me zyada tar shares ki prices badh rahi hoti hain aur investors positive hote hain, use Bull Market kaha jata hai.

Bear Market

Jab market me prices gir rahi hoti hain aur investors ka confidence kam hota hai, use Bear Market kaha jata hai.

Stock Price Kyu Badhta Aur Ghatta Hai?

Stock price mainly demand aur supply ke according change hota hai.

  • Company ke achhe results se price badh sakta hai.
  • Business problems ya negative news se price gir sakta hai.
  • Market conditions aur investor sentiment ka bhi effect hota hai.

Beginner Ki Common Mistakes

  • Bina knowledge ke investment karna.
  • Sirf kisi ke kehne par shares kharidna.
  • Short term profit ke chakkar me jaldi decision lena.
  • Risk management ignore karna.
  • Market ko samjhe bina emotional trading karna.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Kya Stock Market Beginners Ke Liye Hai?

Haan, beginners bhi Stock Market seekh sakte hain. Lekin pehle basics aur risk management samajhna zaroori hai.

2. Kya Stock Market Me Paise Lagana Risky Hai?

Haan, market me risk hota hai. Isliye research aur proper planning ke saath decision lena chahiye.

3. Share Kharidne Ke Liye Kya Chahiye?

Share kharidne ke liye aam taur par Demat Account, Trading Account aur broker ki zarurat hoti hai.

4. Kya Stock Market Se Profit Ho Sakta Hai?

Haan, knowledge aur discipline ke saath investors market opportunities ka benefit le sakte hain.

Conclusion

Ab aap samajh gaye honge ki Stock Market kya hota hai aur ye kaise kaam karta hai. Stock Market companies ko grow karne ka mauka deta hai aur investors ko ownership me participate karne ka chance deta hai.

Beginner ke liye sabse important cheez hai ki pehle learning par focus karein, phir planning aur risk management ke saath market me aage badhein.
