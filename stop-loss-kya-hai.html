Stop Loss Kya Hota Hai?

Trading Mein Risk Control Karne Ki Beginner Guide

Introduction

Trading mein har trade profit mein nahi jaata.

Market kabhi bhi trader ke expectation ke opposite move kar sakta hai.

Isliye ek smart trader pehle se decide karta hai ki agar trade galat hua to kitna loss accept karna hai.

Isi planning ko Stop Loss kaha jata hai.

Stop Loss Kya Hota Hai?

Stop Loss ek aisa order hota hai jo trader ko bade loss se bachane mein help karta hai.

Simple shabdon mein:

Stop Loss ek predefined price level hota hai jahan pahuchne par trader apni position ko exit kar deta hai.

Real Life Stock Market Example

Maan lo kisi trader ne ek stock ₹200 par buy kiya.

Usne decide kiya:

  • Entry Price = ₹200
  • Stop Loss = ₹190

Agar stock ka price ₹190 tak gir jata hai, to trader apna loss limit karne ke liye position close kar sakta hai.

Possible Loss:

₹200 - ₹190 = ₹10 per share

Note: Ye sirf learning example hai, koi trading recommendation nahi hai.

Stop Loss Kyon Important Hota Hai?

1. Capital Protect Karne Mein

Agar trade galat ho jaye to Stop Loss loss ko control mein rakhne mein help karta hai.

2. Emotions Control Karne Mein

Trader bina emotion ke pehle se decide kiye plan ke hisaab se exit kar sakta hai.

3. Risk Management Mein

Har trade ka risk pehle se pata hota hai aur planning better hoti hai.

Stop Loss Ke Types

1. Fixed Stop Loss

Fixed Stop Loss mein trader pehle se ek fixed price decide karta hai jahan pahuchne par position exit karni hai.

Example:

  • Buy Price = ₹200
  • Stop Loss = ₹190

Agar price ₹190 tak aa jata hai, to trader apna loss limit karne ke liye exit kar sakta hai.

2. Trailing Stop Loss

Trailing Stop Loss mein stop loss price ke saath move karta hai.

Iska purpose ye hota hai ki agar trade profit mein ja raha hai to profit ko protect kiya ja sake.

Example:

Agar koi stock ₹200 se badhkar ₹220 ho gaya, to trader apna stop loss upar la sakta hai taaki agar price reverse ho to profit protect rahe.

Stop Loss Kahan Lagana Chahiye?

Stop Loss decide karte waqt trader ko kuch important cheezein dekhni chahiye:

  • Support aur Resistance level
  • Market volatility
  • Trading strategy
  • Risk Reward Ratio

Stop Loss na bahut paas hona chahiye ki chhoti movement mein hit ho jaye aur na itna door ki unnecessary bada loss ho.

Stop Loss Aur Trading Discipline

Stop Loss trader ko discipline maintain karne mein help karta hai.

Kai beginners loss wali trade ko ye sochkar hold karte rehte hain ki price wapas aa jayega.

Lekin ek planned Stop Loss trader ko emotions ke bajaye apni strategy ke hisaab se decision lene mein help karta hai.

Beginners Ki Common Mistakes

  • Stop Loss na lagana.
  • Loss wali trade ko hope ke saath hold karna.
  • Baar-baar Stop Loss change karna.
  • Apni risk capacity ignore karna.
  • Bina plan ke trade lena.

Conclusion

Stop Loss trading ka ek important risk management tool hai.

Ek successful trader sirf profit kamane par focus nahi karta, balki apne capital ko protect karna bhi seekhta hai.

Trading mein discipline, planning aur risk management long-term success ke liye bahut zaroori hain.
