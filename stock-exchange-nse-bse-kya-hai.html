Stock Exchange (NSE & BSE) Kya Hai?

Complete Beginner Guide (2026)

Introduction

Agar aap stock market mein trading ya investing shuru karna chahte hain, to aapne NSE aur BSE ka naam zarur suna hoga. Lekin bahut se beginners ko ye nahi pata hota ki Stock Exchange kya hota hai aur shares ki buying aur selling yahan kaise hoti hai.

Is article mein hum samjhenge Stock Exchange kya hota hai, NSE aur BSE kya hain aur trading mein iska kya role hai.

Stock Exchange Kya Hota Hai?

Stock Exchange ek organized marketplace hota hai jahan companies ke shares, bonds aur anya securities ki buying aur selling hoti hai.

Stock Exchange ek aisi jagah hai jahan buyers aur sellers shares ka transaction karte hain.

Aaj ke samay mein stock exchange par saari trading online hoti hai.

Stock Exchange Ka Kaam Kya Hai?

  • Shares buy aur sell karne ka platform provide karna.
  • Demand aur supply ke hisaab se price decide karna.
  • Safe aur transparent trading provide karna.
  • Companies ko IPO ke through fund raise karne mein madad karna.

India Ke Do Bade Stock Exchange

1. NSE (National Stock Exchange)

NSE India ka ek bada stock exchange hai.

  • Established: 1992
  • Benchmark Index: Nifty 50
  • Electronic trading ke liye famous
  • High trading volume ke liye jana jata hai

2. BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange)

BSE Asia ka sabse purana stock exchange hai.

  • Established: 1875
  • Benchmark Index: Sensex
  • Bahut saari companies yahan listed hain

Stock Exchange Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

  1. Aap broker ke app par order place karte hain.
  2. Broker order ko Stock Exchange tak bhejta hai.
  3. Stock Exchange buyer aur seller ke orders match karta hai.
  4. Trade complete hone ke baad shares Demat Account mein aa jate hain.

Kya Ek Share NSE Aur BSE Dono Par Ho Sakta Hai?

Haan. Bahut si companies ke shares NSE aur BSE dono exchanges par listed hote hain.

Example:

  • Reliance Industries
  • TCS
  • Infosys
  • HDFC Bank

Stock Exchange Aur Broker Mein Difference

Broker: Aapka order Stock Exchange tak pahunchata hai.

Stock Exchange: Jahan actual buying aur selling hoti hai.

Example:

Aap order dete ho → Broker → NSE/BSE → Trade Complete

Common Mistakes

  • Broker aur Stock Exchange ko same samajhna.
  • Bina research ke shares kharidna.
  • Sirf price dekhkar investment karna.
  • Market news ko ignore karna.

Frequently Asked Questions

NSE ka full form National Stock Exchange hai.

BSE ka full form kya hai?

BSE ka full form Bombay Stock Exchange hai.

Kya Stock Exchange par directly trading kar sakte hain?

Nahi. Trading ke liye broker aur Trading Account ki zarurat hoti hai.

Nifty aur Sensex kya hote hain?

Nifty aur Sensex stock market ke important indexes hain jo market performance ko show karte hain.

Conclusion

Stock Exchange stock market ka ek important part hai. Yahi wo platform hai jahan buyers aur sellers shares ki buying aur selling karte hain.

Agar aap trading ya investing seekh rahe hain, to NSE, BSE aur Stock Exchange ka role samajhna bahut zaroori hai.
