Trading Volume Kya Hota Hai?

Volume Analysis Beginner Guide

Introduction

Trading mein aksar log sirf share ka price dekhte hain.

Lekin market ko samajhne ke liye ek aur important cheez hoti hai:

Volume

Volume batata hai ki kisi stock mein kitni buying aur selling ho rahi hai.

Price ke saath volume ko samajhne se trader ko market ki strength aur activity ka idea mil sakta hai.

Trading Volume Kya Hota Hai?

Trading Volume ka matlab hota hai kisi particular time period mein kitne shares buy aur sell hue.

Simple shabdon mein:

Volume batata hai ki market mein kisi stock ki activity kitni zyada ya kam hai.

Example:

Agar kisi stock mein ek din mein 10 lakh shares buy aur sell hue, to us din ka trading volume 10 lakh shares hoga.

Volume Kyon Important Hota Hai?

Volume trader ko market movement ko samajhne mein help kar sakta hai.

  • Price movement strong hai ya weak.
  • Buyers active hain ya sellers.
  • Breakout genuine ho sakta hai ya nahi.
  • Market mein interest badh raha hai ya kam ho raha hai.

Real Life Stock Market Example

Maan lo kisi company ke share ka price ₹100 se badhkar ₹120 ho gaya.

Situation 1:

Price ₹120 tak gaya aur volume bhi bahut zyada tha.

Iska matlab ho sakta hai ki bahut saare buyers interested hain aur movement strong ho sakti hai.

Situation 2:

Price ₹120 tak gaya lekin volume bahut kam tha.

Iska matlab ho sakta hai ki price movement mein utni strength nahi hai.

Note: Ye sirf learning example hai, koi trading recommendation nahi.

Price Aur Volume Ka Relation

Traders aksar price aur volume dono ko saath mein dekhte hain, kyunki dono milkar market activity ko samajhne mein help karte hain.

Price Up + High Volume

Jab price upar ja raha ho aur volume bhi zyada ho, to ye buyers ki strong activity dikha sakta hai.

Price Down + High Volume

Jab price neeche ja raha ho aur volume zyada ho, to ye strong selling pressure ko dikha sakta hai.

Price Move + Low Volume

Agar price move kar raha hai lekin volume kam hai, to movement weak ho sakti hai.

Volume Indicators

Trading mein kuch tools volume ko analyze karne mein help karte hain.

1. Volume Bars

Volume Bars chart ke neeche bars ke form mein dikhte hain aur batate hain ki kis time period mein kitni trading hui.

2. Volume Moving Average

Ye average volume ko compare karne mein help karta hai aur batata hai ki current volume normal hai ya zyada.

3. OBV (On Balance Volume)

OBV price aur volume ke relation ko samajhne ke liye use kiya jata hai.

Breakout Mein Volume Ka Role

Jab price kisi important resistance level ko todta hai, to traders volume ko bhi check karte hain.

Example:

Agar koi stock ₹150 ke resistance level ke upar jata hai aur volume bhi strong hai, to traders ise strong buying interest ke roop mein dekh sakte hain.

Lekin sirf volume ke basis par trade decision nahi lena chahiye.

Beginners Ki Common Mistakes

  • Sirf price dekhna aur volume ignore karna.
  • Har high volume ko buying signal samajhna.
  • Market trend ko ignore karna.
  • Risk management follow na karna.
  • Bina practice ke real money se trading karna.
