Support Aur Resistance Kya Hote Hain?

Trading Mein Important Price Levels Ko Samajhne Ki Beginner Guide

Introduction

Trading mein sirf share ka price dekhna kaafi nahi hota.

Ek trader ko ye samajhna hota hai ki price kahan se wapas upar aa sakta hai aur kahan selling pressure aa sakta hai.

Inhi important price levels ko samajhne ke liye Support aur Resistance ka use kiya jata hai.

Support Kya Hota Hai?

Support ek aisa price level hota hai jahan price girne ke baad aksar buyers active ho jate hain aur price ko neeche jaane se rokne ki koshish karte hain.

Simple shabdon mein:

Support wo level hai jahan demand badhne lagti hai aur price ko sahara mil sakta hai.

Example:

Maan lo kisi share ka price baar-baar ₹100 ke paas aata hai aur wahan se wapas upar chala jata hai.

To ₹100 ek Support Level ki tarah kaam kar sakta hai.

Resistance Kya Hota Hai?

Resistance ek aisa price level hota hai jahan price badhne ke baad aksar sellers active ho jate hain aur price ko upar jaane mein dikkat aa sakti hai.

Simple shabdon mein:

Resistance wo level hai jahan selling pressure badh sakta hai.

Example:

Agar koi share baar-baar ₹150 ke paas jaakar neeche aa jata hai, to ₹150 ek Resistance Level ki tarah kaam kar sakta hai.

Real Life Stock Market Example: Support Aur Resistance Samjho

Maan lo kisi company ke share ka price kuch samay se ₹100 ke aas-paas aata hai aur har baar wahan se wapas upar chala jata hai.

Iska matlab ho sakta hai ki is price level par buyers active ho rahe hain aur buying support mil raha hai.

Isliye ₹100 ek Support Level ki tarah kaam kar sakta hai.

Ab maan lo wahi share baar-baar ₹150 ke paas jaata hai aur wahan se neeche aa jata hai.

Iska matlab ho sakta hai ki is level par sellers active ho rahe hain aur selling pressure aa raha hai.

Isliye ₹150 ek Resistance Level ki tarah kaam kar sakta hai.

⚠️ Ye sirf learning example hai. Real trading decision lene se pehle company analysis, market condition aur risk management ko zaroor samajhna chahiye.

Support Aur Resistance Kaise Identify Karein?

Traders chart par kuch important points ko dekhkar support aur resistance levels identify karte hain.

1. Previous High Aur Low

Purane price levels jahan share baar-baar rukta hai, wo future mein important levels ban sakte hain.

Agar price kisi level se baar-baar upar ya neeche react karta hai, to traders us level ko important maan sakte hain.

2. Multiple Touch Points

Jitni baar price kisi ek level se react karta hai, wo level utna zyada important ho sakta hai.

Example:

Agar koi share teen baar ₹100 ke paas aakar wapas upar chala gaya, to ₹100 ek strong support zone ho sakta hai.

3. Trading Volume

Volume batata hai ki kisi price level par kitni buying aur selling hui.

Agar kisi support ya resistance level par volume zyada ho, to traders us level ko zyada importance de sakte hain.

Support Zone Aur Resistance Zone

Trading mein support aur resistance hamesha ek exact line nahi hote.

Kai baar ye ek zone ki tarah kaam karte hain jahan price react karta hai.

Isliye traders ek single price ke bajaye ek range ko bhi consider karte hain.

Support Aur Resistance Breakout Kya Hota Hai?

Kabhi-kabhi price apne important support ya resistance level ko tod deta hai. Is situation ko breakout ya breakdown kaha jata hai.

Resistance Breakout

Jab share ka price kisi strong resistance level ke upar chala jata hai, to ise resistance breakout kaha jata hai.

Iska matlab ho sakta hai ki buyers strong ho rahe hain aur price mein nayi movement aa sakti hai.

Example:

Agar koi share baar-baar ₹150 ke level par ruk raha tha, lekin baad mein ₹150 ke upar strong buying ke saath chala gaya, to ₹150 resistance breakout ho sakta hai.

Support Breakdown

Jab share ka price kisi important support level ke neeche chala jata hai, to ise support breakdown kaha jata hai.

Iska matlab ho sakta hai ki sellers ka pressure badh raha hai.

Example:

Agar koi share baar-baar ₹100 ke support par tik raha tha, lekin baad mein ₹100 ke neeche chala gaya, to ye support breakdown ho sakta hai.

Resistance Support Mein Kaise Badal Sakta Hai?

Technical Analysis mein kai baar ek purana resistance level baad mein support ki tarah kaam kar sakta hai.

Example:

Maan lo kisi share ka resistance ₹150 tha.

Agar price ₹150 ke upar breakout karta hai aur baad mein wapas ₹150 ke paas aata hai, to kabhi-kabhi wahi level support ki tarah kaam kar sakta hai.

Is concept ko traders aksar "Resistance Turned Into Support" kehte hain.

Trading Mein Support Aur Resistance Ka Use

Traders support aur resistance ka use kai decisions lene ke liye karte hain.

  • Entry point samajhne ke liye.
  • Stop Loss decide karne ke liye.
  • Target price plan karne ke liye.
  • Risk aur Reward samajhne ke liye.

Example:

Agar koi trader support level ke paas entry leta hai, to wo pehle se decide kar sakta hai ki agar price support ke neeche chala gaya to uska exit point kya hoga.

Isse trading mein discipline maintain karne mein help milti hai.

Risk Management Ka Importance

Support aur Resistance useful tools hain, lekin koi bhi level 100% guarantee nahi deta.

Kabhi-kabhi strong support bhi break ho sakta hai aur resistance bhi toot sakta hai.

Isliye trader ko hamesha:

  • Stop Loss ka use karna chahiye.
  • Apna risk pehle decide karna chahiye.
  • Emotion ke basis par trade nahi lena chahiye.

Beginners Ki Common Mistakes

Support aur Resistance ko samajhte waqt beginners kuch common mistakes karte hain.

  • Har price level ko support ya resistance samajhna.
  • Sirf ek level dekhkar trade decision lena.
  • Breakout ko confirm nahi karna.
  • Volume aur market trend ko ignore karna.
  • Stop Loss ka use nahi karna.

Ek smart trader hamesha multiple factors ko combine karke decision leta hai.

Conclusion

Support aur Resistance Technical Analysis ke bahut important concepts hain.

Ye traders ko market ke important price zones samajhne mein help karte hain jahan buying aur selling pressure aa sakta hai.

Lekin successful trading ke liye sirf support aur resistance par depend nahi karna chahiye.

Ek responsible trader hamesha:

  • Market trend ko samajhta hai.
  • Risk management follow karta hai.
  • Discipline ke saath decision leta hai.

Learning, practice aur experience ke saath hi trading skills improve hoti hain.
