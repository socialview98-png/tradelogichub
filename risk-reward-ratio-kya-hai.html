Risk Reward Ratio Kya Hota Hai?

Trading Mein Risk Aur Reward Ko Samajhne Ki Beginner Guide

Introduction

Trading mein har trade mein profit aur loss dono ka chance hota hai.

Ek beginner aksar sirf ye sochta hai ki share kitna upar ja sakta hai aur kitna profit hoga.

Lekin ek experienced trader ye bhi sochta hai ki agar trade galat hua to kitna loss hoga aur possible profit kitna ho sakta hai.

Isi concept ko samajhne ke liye Risk Reward Ratio ka use kiya jata hai.

Risk Reward Ratio Kya Hota Hai?

Risk Reward Ratio batata hai ki trader ek trade mein kitna risk le raha hai aur uske badle mein kitna potential reward expect kar raha hai.

Simple shabdon mein:

Risk Reward Ratio potential loss aur potential profit ke beech ka comparison hota hai.

Risk Kya Hota Hai?

Risk ka matlab hota hai wo amount jo trader ek trade mein lose kar sakta hai.

Example:

Agar kisi trader ne ek stock ₹100 par buy kiya aur stop loss ₹95 rakha:

  • Entry Price = ₹100
  • Stop Loss = ₹95

Risk calculation:

₹100 - ₹95 = ₹5

Matlab trader ka risk ₹5 per share hai.

Reward Kya Hota Hai?

Reward ka matlab hota hai wo potential profit jo trader target price tak pahuchne par kama sakta hai.

Example:

Agar wahi stock ₹100 par buy kiya aur target ₹110 rakha:

  • Entry Price = ₹100
  • Target Price = ₹110

Reward calculation:

₹110 - ₹100 = ₹10

Risk Reward Ratio Kaise Calculate Karte Hain?

Risk Reward Ratio potential loss aur potential profit ke comparison se calculate kiya jata hai.

Formula:

Risk Reward Ratio = Potential Loss : Potential Profit

Example:

  • Risk = ₹5
  • Reward = ₹10

Ratio:

₹5 : ₹10

Simplify karne par:

1 : 2

Matlab trader ₹1 risk lekar ₹2 ke potential reward
