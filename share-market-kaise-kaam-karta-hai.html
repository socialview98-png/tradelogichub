Share Market Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

Complete Beginner Guide (2026)

Introduction

Bahut se log share market ka naam sunte hain, lekin unhe ye nahi pata hota ki actual mein share market kaise kaam karta hai.

Share market ke peeche ek poora system kaam karta hai jisme companies, investors, traders, brokers, stock exchanges aur regulators ka important role hota hai.

Is article mein hum samjhenge ki share market kya hota hai aur iska poora system kaise kaam karta hai.

Share Market Kya Hota Hai?

Share Market ek aisa marketplace hai jahan companies ke shares investors ke dwara buy aur sell kiye jaate hain.

Jab koi investor kisi company ka share kharidta hai, to wo us company ka chhota sa hissa ka owner ban jata hai.

Example: Agar kisi company ke 1,00,000 shares hain aur aapke paas us company ke 100 shares hain, to aap bhi us company ke ek chhote shareholder hain.

Company Shares Kyun Bechti Hai?

Companies ko business grow karne ke liye paison ki zarurat hoti hai.

  • Business expansion ke liye
  • Naye projects ke liye
  • Technology improve karne ke liye
  • Growth ke liye capital raise karne ke liye

Jab company pehli baar public ko shares offer karti hai, use IPO (Initial Public Offering) kaha jata hai.

Share Market Mein Kaun-Kaun Hote Hain?

1. Companies

Companies apne shares issue karti hain taaki wo fund raise kar saken.

2. Investors

Investors shares kharidte hain aur company ki growth se benefit lene ki koshish karte hain.

3. Traders

Traders short-term price movement ka fayda lene ki koshish karte hain.

4. Stock Broker

Broker investor aur Stock Exchange ke beech connection ka kaam karta hai.

5. Stock Exchange

NSE aur BSE jaise exchanges par shares ki buying aur selling hoti hai.

6. SEBI

SEBI market ko regulate karta hai aur rules banata hai taaki market transparent rahe.

Share Market Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

  1. Investor Trading Account se buy ya sell order deta hai.
  2. Broker order ko Stock Exchange tak bhejta hai.
  3. Stock Exchange buyer aur seller ke orders match karta hai.
  4. Trade complete hone ke baad shares Demat Account mein aa jate hain.

Share Ki Price Kaise Decide Hoti Hai?

Share ki price koi ek insaan decide nahi karta.

Market mein demand aur supply ke hisaab se price badhti aur ghat-ti hai.

Demand Zyada

Agar kisi share ko kharidne wale log zyada hain aur bechne wale kam hain, to share ki price badh sakti hai.

Supply Zyada

Agar bahut saare log share bechna chahte hain aur buyers kam hain, to share ki price gir sakti hai.

Real Life Example: Apple (Fruit) Se Samjho

Maan lo ek fruit market mein apple bik raha hai.

Normal din par apple ki supply aur demand balance hai aur apple ka rate $2 per kg hai.

Agar ek din bahut saare log apple kharidna chahte hain lekin market mein apple kam hain, to demand badh jayegi.

Buyers zyada hone ki wajah se apple ki price badh sakti hai.

Ab agar market mein bahut saare apple aa gaye lekin kharidne wale kam hain, to supply zyada ho jayegi aur price gir sakti hai.

Share market mein bhi demand aur supply ke wajah se shares ki price change hoti hai.

  • Demand zyada = Price badhne ka chance
  • Supply zyada = Price girne ka chance

Bull Market Aur Bear Market

Bull Market

Jab market mein shares ki prices generally badh rahi hoti hain, use Bull Market kaha jata hai.

Bear Market

Jab market mein shares ki prices generally gir rahi hoti hain, use Bear Market kaha jata hai.

Share Market Se Log Kaise Kamaane Ki Koshish Karte Hain?

1. Long Term Investing

Investor achhi companies ke shares kharidkar lambe samay tak hold karte hain.

2. Trading

Trader short-term price movement ka fayda lene ki koshish karte hain.
