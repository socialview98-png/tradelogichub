Risk Reward Formula

Risk Per Share = Entry Price − Stop Loss

Reward Per Share = Target Price − Entry Price

Risk Reward Ratio = Reward ÷ Risk

Position Size = Risk Amount ÷ Risk Per Share

How To Read Risk Reward Ratio

  • 1 : 1 → Poor Trade
  • 1 : 1.5 → Average Trade
  • 1 : 2 → Good Trade
  • 1 : 3+ → Excellent Trade

Professional Risk Management Tips

  • Never trade without a Stop Loss.
  • Risk only 1–2% of your capital per trade.
  • Maintain a minimum Risk Reward Ratio of 1:2.
  • Do not increase quantity after taking losses.
  • Follow your trading plan with discipline.

Common Trading Mistakes

  • Ignoring Stop Loss.
  • Taking random entries.
  • Overtrading.
  • Using excessive leverage.
  • Trading based on emotions.
  • Ignoring Risk Reward Ratio.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good Risk Reward Ratio?

Most professional traders prefer at least a 1:2 Risk Reward Ratio.

Can beginners use this calculator?

Does this calculator provide Buy or Sell signals?

Why is Position Size important?

Position sizing helps control losses and protects trading capital over the long term.
