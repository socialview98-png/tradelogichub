Risk Reward Formula

Risk Reward Ratio calculate karne ke liye sirf do simple formulas use hote hain.

Risk = Entry Price − Stop Loss

Reward = Target Price − Entry Price

Risk Reward Ratio = Reward ÷ Risk

Professional traders generally pehle Risk aur Reward calculate karte hain, uske baad hi trade enter karte hain.

Example Calculation

Entry Price : ₹100

Stop Loss : ₹95

Target : ₹115

Risk = ₹5

Reward = ₹15

Risk Reward Ratio = 1 : 3

Is example me trader sirf ₹5 ka risk lekar ₹15 ka reward expect kar raha hai.

What is a Good Risk Reward Ratio?

Har strategy alag hoti hai, lekin kai traders generally 1:2 ya usse better Risk Reward Ratio ko prefer karte hain.

  • 1 : 1 → Minimum
  • 1 : 2 → Good
  • 1 : 3 → Very Good
  • 1 : 4+ → Excellent (agar strategy support kare)

High Risk Reward Ratio hone se bhi profit guarantee nahi hota. Trade quality aur strategy equally important hoti hai.

Why Risk Reward Ratio is Important?

Risk Reward Ratio trader ko impulsive trading se bachata hai.

  • Capital Protection
  • Better Trade Planning
  • Controlled Losses
  • Consistent Decision Making
  • Improved Trading Discipline

Common Mistakes

  • Stop Loss decide kiye bina trade lena.
  • Target randomly set karna.
  • Risk Reward calculate na karna.
  • Loss hone par Stop Loss ko aur neeche shift karna.
  • Emotion me trade execute karna.

Professional Trading Tips

  • Har trade se pehle Risk Reward calculate karein.
  • Trading journal maintain karein.
  • Maximum risk per trade pehle decide karein.
  • Sirf achhe setups me trade karein.
  • Emotions ki jagah rules follow karein.
