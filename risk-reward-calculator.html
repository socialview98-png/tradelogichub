Risk Reward Calculator Kya Hai?

Risk Reward Calculator ek trading tool hai jo trader ko trade lene se pehle ye samajhne mein help karta hai ki wo kitna risk le raha hai aur uske badle potential reward kitna ho sakta hai.

Professional traders aksar trade enter karne se pehle apna entry price, stop loss aur target decide karte hain taaki risk ko control kiya ja sake.

Risk Reward Ratio kya hota hai?

Risk Reward Ratio batata hai ki ek trade mein possible loss ke comparison mein expected profit kitna hai.

Example: Agar aap kisi trade mein ₹10 ka risk le rahe hain aur possible reward ₹20 hai, to Risk Reward Ratio 1:2 hoga.

Calculator Kaise Use Kare?

Step 1 — Entry Price Dale

Jis price par aap trade enter karna chahte hain, wo entry price box mein dale.

Step 2 — Stop Loss Dale

Wo price dale jahan aap maximum loss ko limit karna chahte hain.

Step 3 — Target Price Dale

Apna expected profit target enter kare aur Calculate button press kare.

Example

Maan lijiye:

  • Entry Price = ₹500
  • Stop Loss = ₹490
  • Target Price = ₹520

Is trade mein risk ₹10 hai aur reward ₹20 hai. Isliye Risk Reward Ratio 1:2 hoga.

Common Trading Mistakes

  • Trade lene se pehle risk calculate na karna.
  • Bina Stop Loss ke trading karna.
  • Sirf profit ke baare mein sochna aur risk ignore karna.
  • Ek hi trade mein bahut zyada capital lagana.
  • Emotion mein aakar trading decisions lena.

Risk Management Tips

  • Har trade se pehle Entry, Stop Loss aur Target decide kare.
  • Risk Reward Ratio ko samajhkar trade plan banaye.
  • Apne trading capital ko protect karna priority rakhe.
  • Trading journal maintain kare aur mistakes se seekhe.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Risk Reward Ratio kya hota hai?

Risk Reward Ratio possible loss aur possible profit ke beech ka comparison hota hai.

2. Kya high Risk Reward Ratio hamesha better hota hai?

Nahi. Ratio ke saath trading strategy, accuracy aur risk management bhi important hote hain.

3. Kya ye calculator buy ya sell signal deta hai?

Nahi. Ye sirf risk aur reward calculate karne mein help karta hai. Ye koi trading recommendation nahi deta.

4. Beginners ke liye Risk Reward Calculator useful hai?

Haan, beginners ko trade lene se pehle apna risk samajhne mein help mil sakti hai.
